### Autopilot-full

This playbook is not available on this platform. It squash-merges, rebases, opens ready pull requests, or needs `gt` or `origin`. Do not follow any step of it.

Use the development loop instead:

- Commit small and often in your own worktree. Never rebase, amend or force-push.
- `dev-loop fold` joins each finished task into the goal branch.
- The owner lands the goal. Stop at merge-ready and report the evidence.

See `docs/source-control.md` and `docs/pstack-platform.md`.
