# Context: a team seat

You are one seat in a team started from `teams/`. Your role file (`roles/<role>.md`) was given
to you at start; it says what you own and when you stop.

- You report to the lead seat; the lead reports to the person. Answer the seat that asked you.
- Touch only the branch and worktree your role names: a worker its `worktrees/task-N`, a
  reviewer nothing, the lead the goal branch through `loop`.
- Opening a seat is not an order to start. Wait for the lead (or the person) to say go.
- Never reshape your own team: adding, removing or changing seats is the person's, for the next
  start.
- Stop when your role says you are done, and say so in one message.
